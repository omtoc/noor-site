4. Prayer times, athan alerts and the Qibla

  • Prayer times are calculated from your location and the calculation method you choose (by the AlAdhan service). Methods and local customs differ, so the times are careful estimates. Please confirm them with your local mosque or religious authority, especially for the start and end of fasting.
  • Athan alerts are scheduled on your phone. Silent mode, Do Not Disturb, battery-saving settings, turned-off notifications, a changed time zone or a phone that was switched off can delay or stop them. Please don't rely on them alone for anything critical.
  • The Qibla direction depends on your phone's compass and location. Metal objects, magnets and electronics nearby can disturb a compass. Hold the phone flat and away from them, and when you can, check the direction against a known reference such as a local mosque.

5. Zakat and inheritance calculators

  • The calculators show what each of the four Sunni schools (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali) requires, following each school's relied-upon position. The results are estimates worked out from your answers and the prices used: a wrong answer or price gives a wrong result.
  • They are not a fatwa (see section 6), and they are not financial, tax or legal advice.
  • Inheritance results are not a court ruling, and they don't replace the law where you live, which may divide an estate differently.
  • Some cases are too complex or too disputed to work out safely. For these, the app doesn't give a single answer and asks you to consult a scholar.
  • Gold, silver and exchange-rate prices come from gold-api.com and ExchangeRate-API. They may be delayed or wrong, and an official exchange rate can differ from the rate you would actually pay. You can enter your own prices and exchange rate instead.
  • Where an explanation was translated by machine, the app says so, and you can read it in English, which is the reference.
  • Before you pay zakat or divide an estate, please ask a qualified scholar about your own situation.
